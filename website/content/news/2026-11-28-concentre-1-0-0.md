Concentré is now 1.0. The app has been used daily for a long time, under its old name and its new one, and this release marks it as stable. It also brings a few improvements described below.

# What’s new

- **A resizable interface.** Make the window bigger or smaller: the whole interface now scales with it and keeps its proportions, instead of growing only with the width.
- **A steadier tick sound.** The ticking during a session is now regular, especially on Windows, where it could sound uneven.
- **Lighter on your computer.** The tray icon is drawn more efficiently and only when it actually changes.
- **Clearer error messages.** When something goes wrong, for example with an unreadable configuration file, the app now tells you why instead of failing silently.
- **Release notes from the app.** After an update, a small dot appears on the heart icon. Click it to read what changed, as you may be doing right now. It only appears once per version and never during a running session.

# Your privacy

The new dot does not need the internet: the app simply remembers the last version whose notes you opened. Concentré still makes no network requests on its own and collects no data.

# What changes for you

Nothing. Your settings are kept, and updates keep coming through the channel you installed the app from.
