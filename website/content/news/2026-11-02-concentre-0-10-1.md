Version 0.10.1 is a small maintenance release that completes the switch from Pomodorolm to Concentré.

# What’s new

- **New icons.** The app icons now carry the Concentré name everywhere, including the Flatpak package.
- **Updated screenshots** on the website and in the app stores, showing the new name.
- **Arch Linux users** can now start the app with the `concentre` command, in addition to the old one.
- **Updated dependencies**, to keep the app secure and up to date.

Your settings are kept, and the update arrives through the channel you installed the app from, as usual.
