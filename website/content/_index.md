# Stay focused, one round at a time

Concentré helps you work in focused rounds, take short breaks between them, and a longer break every few rounds. It stays out of your way in the tray, and tells you when it’s time to switch.

# See time passing

The timer circle slowly empties and shifts color as time runs out, and the tray icon does the same, even when the window is hidden. You always know where you are in your round without reading a number, which helps a lot if you struggle to feel time passing (often described as *time blindness*, common with ADHD).

# Features

- **Customize** round numbers, focus and break durations
- **Labels** for the current and default sessions
- **Auto-start** of the next round (optional)
- **Desktop notifications** (optional)
- **Built-in and custom themes**
- **Color gradient** as the remaining time decreases, also on the **tray icon**
- **Minimize to tray** and **always on top** (optional)
- **Tick and end sounds**, including your own sound files
- **Resizable window** with automatic scaling, HiDPI/4K ready
- **Linux**: Wayland and X11 support

# Light by design

Concentré is built with [Tauri](https://tauri.app), [Rust](https://www.rust-lang.org) and [Elm](https://elm-lang.org). No Electron, no bundled browser: the whole app weighs less than 4 MB.

# Free and open source

Concentré is free software, developed in the open on [GitHub](https://github.com/vjousse/concentre). If it helps you stay focused, you can [support its development on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/vjousse).
